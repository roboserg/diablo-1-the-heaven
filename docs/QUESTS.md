# Quests Guide

| Quest | Area | DLVL | Objective | Reward |
|-------|------|------|-----------|--------|
| **Butcher** | Church | 1 | Descend to Level 1 and hunt down The Butcher in his special chamber. Kill him to complete the quest. | Magic Items |
| **Iron Oath** | Church | 1 | Travel to the Church Sacristy (a side area off Level 1). Locate and pick up the Dusty Crate, then return it to Griswold in town. | Gold |
| **Poisoned Water Supply** | Church | 2 | Find the Poisoned Water Supply dungeon entrance on Level 2 and enter it. Clear the poisoned cavern to cleanse the water source. | Ring of Purity |
| **Grave Matters** | Church | 2 | Locate the Black Chapel entrance on Level 2. Descend and defeat the Ghost of Brother Martin lurking inside. | Magic Items |
| **The Curse of King Leoric** | Church | 3 | Find King Leoric's Tomb on Level 3 and enter it. Defeat King Leoric to break the curse. | Magic Items |
| **A Demon in Town** | Church | 4 | Search town for a randomly placed home called "Sick Villager's Tomb". Descend into the basement beneath it and kill the demon hiding inside. | Magic Items |
| **Ogden's Sign** | Church | 4 | Find Ogden's Chest on Level 4. Retrieve the sign from it and return it to Ogden in town. *(SP only)* | Peasant's Cap |
| **Magic Rock** | Catacombs | 5 | Explore Level 5 and find the Magic Rock table object hidden somewhere in the catacombs. Interact with it to complete the quest. | Empyrean Band |
| **Valor** | Catacombs | 5 | Find the Book of Blood on Level 5 and read it. This opens the way to the Gateway of Blood. Enter and defeat the Apparition of Arkaine. *(SP only)* | Arkaine's Valor |
| **The Chamber of Bone** | Catacombs | 6 | Locate the Mythical Book on Level 6 and interact with it to open the way to the Hall of Heroes. Clear the Hall of Heroes to complete the quest. | Learn a Spell |
| **Rampaging Demon** | Catacombs | 6 | Talk to Ogden in town to start. Clear Level 6, then venture deeper into the Den and keep killing monsters until the demon boss spawns. Return the severed head to Ogden in town. *(SP only)* | Gold |
| **Halls of the Blind** | Catacombs | 7 | Find the Book of the Blind on Level 7 and read it. This opens a hidden area in the level. Clear the revealed section to complete the quest. | Amulet of the Brune |
| **Lost Treasure** | Catacombs | 7 | Obtain the Treasure Map from town or a monster drop on Level 7. Use the map — it will teleport you to the Disused Reliquary. Navigate the Reliquary and defeat the Lich King boss inside. | Magic Items |
| **Zhar the Mad** | Catacombs | 8 | Find Zhar the Mad hidden in the catacombs on Level 8. Talk to him to complete the quest. | Identify Scroll |
| **Good Deal** | Catacombs | 8 | Find the Vaultkeep dungeon entrance on Level 8 and descend into it. Defeat the boss Grimwing inside. Alternatively, find "The Right Goods" item and return it to Griswold in town. *(SP only)* | Gold |
| **Infested Cellar** | Caves | 9 | Find the cellar entrance near Farnham outside in town. Descend and clear the infested cellar of all creatures inside. | Magic Items |
| **Demon Plague** | Caves | 9 | Find the Mossy Grotto entrance on Level 9 and enter it. Defeat the boss Lichen Axe the Fungal inside. Alternatively, pick up the Reagents that drop and return them to town for a unique item reward. | Talisman of Mending |
| **Anvil of Fury** | Caves | 10 | Find the Anvil of Fury on Level 10. In single player, pick it up to claim your class-specific unique weapon. In multiplayer, defeat the Warden of Anger boss guarding the area instead. | Griswold's Edge |
| **Island of the Sunless Seat** | Caves | 11 | Enter the Island dungeon on Level 11. Find the angel statues and click them in the correct random order — the statue that casts Flash and damages you is correct. 9 correct clicks in sequence completes the quest; any wrong click resets your progress. | Magic Items |
| **Clear Water** | Caves | 11 | Pick up the Empty Container from Pepin in town. Travel to Level 11 and enter the Still Springs dungeon. Fill the container at the water source inside, then return the Full Containers to town and use them. | Random Stat Elixir |
| **Gharbad the Weak** | Caves | 11 | Find Gharbad lurking in the dungeon on Level 11. Talk to him, then talk to him again to receive his promised reward. | Magic Items |
| **Black Mushroom** | Caves | 12 | On Level 12, find a patch of mushrooms or a Fungal Tome. Take it to Adria in town — she will give you the Big Black Mushroom. Use it for a stat bonus. | +5 to All Stats (non-Classic) |
| **Maiden of Anguish** | Caves | 12 | Find the Halls of Anguish dungeon accessible from Level 12. Inside, locate and defeat Andariel the demon boss. | Stats |
| **Iron Figure** | Caves | 12 | Find the Fetid Cavern entrance on Level 12. Descend and defeat the boss Mudsnout inside. | Empyrean Bracers |
| **Warlord of Blood** | Hell | 13 | Find and defeat the Warlord of Blood on Level 13. *(SP only)* | Magic Items |
| **Price of Hubris** | Hell | 13 | Find the entrance to The Maze on Level 13. Enter and defeat the boss Borosteron inside. | Magic Items |
| **Lachdanan** | Hell | 14 | Find Lachdanan on Level 14 and talk to him — he will give you the Golden Elixir. Talk to him again after using it to claim your reward. *(SP only)* | Veil of Steel (class-specific) |
| **The Cursed Blade** | Hell | 14 | Find the Pit of Flame on Level 14 and defeat Fleshdoom to open the path forward. Descend into the City of the Damned and defeat the Wielder of Shadowfang to complete the quest. | Magic Items |
| **Archbishop Lazarus** | Hell | 15 | Find Archbishop Lazarus's Lair (the Unholy Altar) on Level 15. Defeat Archbishop Lazarus to open a Red Portal and unlock the final battle. | Traitorous Apparel (mage classes) |
| **Diablo** | Hell | 15 | Defeat Archbishop Lazarus first, then confront Diablo on Level 15. Killing Diablo ends the game. | Victory |
| **Celia's Toy** | Abyss | 17 | Find the toy Theodore somewhere in the dungeons. Bring it to Celia to complete the quest. *(SP only)* | Class-specific Unique Item |
| **Strange Book** | Abyss | 17 | Find the Web Tunnels entrance on Level 17. Enter and defeat the Web Queen boss inside. *(Ironman / Nightmare / Survival / Speedrun only)* | Access to Arachnid Lair |
| **The Locked Chest** | Abyss | 18 | Find the Dark Nest dungeon on Level 18. Inside, locate both the Locked Chest and the Chest Key. Use the key to open the chest. | Unique Item + Stone |
| **The Fallen Angel** | Abyss | 19 | Find the Passage of Fire or Valley of Despair on Level 19. Locate and defeat Izual to complete the quest. | Class-specific Reward |
| **Special Weapon** | Abyss | 20 | Find the Blood Hive dungeon on Level 20. Enter and defeat the Hive Mother boss inside. | Class-specific Unique Weapon |
| **A Simple Task** | Crypt | 21 | Find the Abandoned Fane dungeon on Level 21. Enter and defeat Sir Valon. | Magic Items |
| **Visions of Victory** | Crypt | 22 | Find the Temple of Sunset dungeon on Level 22. Enter and defeat Bloodhoof Twinaxe. | Magic Items |
| **Horazon's Demons** | Crypt | 23 | Find Horazon's Haven on Level 23. Navigate through the Summoning Rooms into the Pit. Defeat Grimspike to open the Pit, then defeat NaKrul in the deepest chamber to complete the quest. | Magic Items |
